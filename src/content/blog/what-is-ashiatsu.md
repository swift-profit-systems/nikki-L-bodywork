# What Is Ashiatsu? A Guide to Barefoot Massage

Most people have never heard of Ashiatsu before their first session. They book because someone told them it was the deepest massage they'd ever had, or because they've spent years looking for something that could actually get there.

Here's what it is.

## The Basics

Ashiatsu is a massage technique where the therapist uses their feet — supported by overhead bars — instead of their hands to deliver pressure to the body. The word itself comes from Japanese: *ashi* (foot) + *atsu* (pressure).

What this creates is something that hands genuinely cannot replicate: broad, sustained, consistent pressure that travels along the full length of a muscle. Where hands create point-specific pressure, feet create a long, gliding compression that reaches into the deepest layers of tissue.

## Why Feet Work Better for Deep Work

The mechanics are straightforward. A therapist's foot covers significantly more surface area than their elbow or thumb. That means more tissue contacted at once, more consistent pressure across the entire length of a muscle, and less concentrated force at any single point.

The result is pressure that feels both deeper and more comfortable than traditional deep tissue work. Many clients describe it as feeling like two therapists working at the same time.

## Who Benefits Most

Ashiatsu is particularly effective for:

- Athletes and active people with dense, layered muscle tissue
- Anyone who has been told they can't be worked on deeply enough with standard technique
- Chronic fascial restrictions that run the length of the back, hamstrings, or glutes
- Post-event recovery or pre-event preparation

It's not the right choice for everyone — certain conditions and sensitivities make Ashiatsu contraindicated. We'll discuss your health history before any session.

## What to Expect

If you've never experienced Ashiatsu, expect to be surprised by how much pressure is possible without discomfort. The overhead bar system lets me control my weight precisely, so the session is fully adjustable to what your body needs.

Most clients leave feeling both deeply worked and unusually relaxed — a combination that's hard to achieve with hands-only technique.
